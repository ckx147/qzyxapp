# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Primary users are children using a playful learning game collection in short sessions, likely with a parent nearby. Parents are secondary users: they need the experience to feel safe, legible, encouraging, and not overly stimulating.

## Product Purpose

Children's Fun Brain Game Park helps kids practice attention, reasoning, memory, and pattern recognition through mini games, daily check-ins, a companion pet, points, achievements, and a simple leaderboard. Success means the app feels joyful and easy to understand while keeping rewards, progress, and navigation clear.

## Brand Personality

Warm, clever, lively. The interface should feel like a polished children's learning toy rather than a generic game portal: playful enough for kids, structured enough for parents to trust.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI gradient dashboards, excessive glass panels, decorative card piles, low-contrast pastel text, casino-like reward pressure, noisy animations, and interfaces that rely on tiny labels or dense adult settings screens.

## Design Principles

- Make the next action obvious for a child without long explanations.
- Keep rewards cheerful but not manipulative or frantic.
- Use strong hierarchy and large touch targets before adding decoration.
- Treat the mobile frame as the real product surface, not as a demo mockup.
- Keep parent trust visible through calm structure, readable copy, and predictable navigation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Preserve reduced-motion alternatives, avoid color-only state changes, keep touch targets comfortable on mobile, and make visual feedback understandable for children who read slowly or rely on icons.
